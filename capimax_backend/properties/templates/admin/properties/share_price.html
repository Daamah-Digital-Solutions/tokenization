What a new price does — immediately:
  • New buyers pay the new price.
  • Every owner's shares are valued at the new price in their portfolio and dashboard.
  • The property value and the minimum investment move in the same proportion (the minimum stays the same number of shares).
  • The change is kept in the price history shown on the property page, and every owner is told in-app and by email.
