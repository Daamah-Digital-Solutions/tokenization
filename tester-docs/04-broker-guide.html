Broker Guide

How to apply as a broker, manage your referred clients, track their investments, see your commissions accrue, request payouts, and use marketing materials.

1. Broker application

Brokers must apply and be approved before they can refer clients. The seeded broker@test.com account is already approved. This section covers the new-broker application path.

  1. Sign up a new account with role = Broker

    OR — from an existing investor account, request a role upgrade in Settings → Roles.

  2. Complete the broker application form

    • Business name
    • License / registration number
    • Country of operation
    • Years of experience
    • Areas of specialty
    • Compliance attestations (checkboxes)
    • Supporting documents (license PDF, etc.)

  3. Submit. Status flips to Under review.

  4. Admin reviews via Django Admin and approves. You receive a notification + email.

  5. Status flips to Approved. You now see the broker dashboard.

3. Referring clients

3.1 Get your referral link

  1. Side nav → Marketing → Referral Links

  2. You see one or more pre-generated referral codes

    Format: https://capimax.com/r/{your-code}

  3. Click Copy link

3.2 Send the link to a prospective investor

When that prospect signs up via your link, they're tagged with your broker_id automatically. They appear in your Clients tab once they complete KYC.

3.3 Manual referral

If a client signed up directly (without using your link), you can manually request that they be assigned to you:

  1. Clients tab → Add Referral

  2. Enter the client's email

  3. Submit. The client receives a confirmation request — they have to accept.

  4. Once accepted, the client appears in your list with status Active

3.4 Tracking a client

Clients tab shows one row per referred investor with:

  • Name
  • Status (Signed up / KYC pending / KYC approved / Active / Inactive)
  • Joined date
  • Total invested
  • Commission earned from this client
  • Last activity

Click a row to drill in. You see the client's investment history at the property level (not the transaction level — that's private).

Privacy boundary

Brokers see aggregate investment data per client, but NOT transaction-level detail and NOT the client's wallet address or payment-method details. If you can see those, file a privacy bug immediately.

4. How commissions work

4.1 Commission structure

Commissions accrue whenever a referred client invests in a property. The platform pays the broker a percentage of the platform fee on each investment.

TierCommission rateRequirement
Bronze10% of platform feeDefault tier for new brokers
Silver15% of platform fee$50,000 referred volume in a quarter
Gold20% of platform fee$250,000 referred volume in a quarter
Platinum25% of platform fee$1,000,000 referred volume in a quarter

Tier is recalculated at the end of each quarter based on the rolling 3-month volume.

4.2 Commission ledger

  1. Side nav → Commissions

  2. You see a paginated table of every commission line

    ColumnMeaning
    DateWhen the investment was made
    ClientName (no email)
    PropertyTitle
    InvestmentAmount invested by the client
    Commission rateTier-based %
    CommissionYour earnings on this line
    StatusAccrued / Paid / Cancelled

  3. Filter by date range, status, property, client

  4. Export to CSV (button top-right)

4.3 Reversals

If a client's investment gets refunded (e.g. terminal mint failure), the corresponding commission line is reversed. The row's status changes to Cancelled and a negative reversal entry is added.

  1. Side nav → Marketing

  2. Browse the materials library

7. Requesting a commission payout

  1. Side nav → Payouts → Request Payout

  2. Form fields:

    • Amount (must be ≤ Pending Payout balance)
    • Payout method (bank, crypto, or platform wallet)
    • Destination details
    • Notes (optional)

  3. Submit. Status: Pending review.

  4. Admin reviews via Django Admin. Once approved, status flips to Processing.

  5. When the off-platform transfer is confirmed, status becomes Paid. You see a transaction reference.

  6. The corresponding commission ledger lines flip from Accrued to Paid.

Minimum payout

In the test environment the minimum payout is $10. In production this is configurable per region. Try requesting under the minimum — should be refused with a clear message.

8. Error paths to test

ScenarioExpected response
Try to invest as a brokerThe platform allows brokers to invest if they have a separate investor role assignment, but blocks self-referral
Refer a client who's already referred by another brokerRejected with "Client already referred"
Manually refer a client who refusesPending state expires after 14 days; never appears in your list
Request payout exceeding pending balanceValidation error
Request payout below minimumValidation error
Request a second payout while one is processingShould queue the second one OR block until the first completes (test which one)
Try to view a client's wallet addressNot exposed; should not be visible anywhere in the broker UI
Try to view another broker's commissions via URL guess403 or empty results
Client's investment fails (mint reverted)Commission line gets cancelled; payable balance decreases
Broker tier downgradeIf you drop below threshold this quarter, tier flips at quarter end; commission rate falls accordingly on future investments

9. Tester checklist

DoneAction
□Apply as a new broker; complete the application; reach Under review
□Have admin approve; status flips to Approved
□Get your referral link; verify it routes to signup
□Sign up an investor via your link; confirm they appear in your Clients
□Trigger an investment from that client (use a different browser/incognito)
□Verify the commission line appears in your ledger with status Accrued
□Force a mint failure on the client's investment; commission line reverses to Cancelled
□Request a payout; admin approves; status reaches Paid
□Download a marketing brochure
□Verify privacy: no email, no wallet address, no payment-method details exposed
□Try to view another broker's data via URL manipulation; expect 403
