Under-Construction Property Lifecycle

End-to-end walkthrough of a single under-construction property, from the owner's first submission through investor installment payments, on-chain token graduation, milestone updates, and project completion. This guide spans every role.

Time: ~75 minutes (sequential)
Prereqs: All four role guides
Test accounts: owner, investor (x2), admin

2. Stage 1: Owner submits property

Browser session A — logged in as owner@test.com.

  1. Owner dashboard → My Properties → Add New Property

  2. Fill Step 1 (Basic info)

    • Title: Marina Tower 12 (Walkthrough)
    • Description: 2 paragraphs of plausible-looking copy
    • Property type: Residential
    • Property category: Under Construction

  3. Step 2 (Financial)

    • Total value: 1200000
    • Token price: 120
    • Total tokens: 10000 (auto-calc)
    • Expected return: 15
    • Rental yield: 0 (will update post-completion)
    • Minimum investment: 120

  4. Step 3 (Property details)

    • Address: 12 Marina Way, Dubai
    • City: Dubai, Country: UAE
    • Property size: 1200 sqm
    • Expected completion date: pick a date 12 months from today
    • Construction progress: 0%
    • Construction phase: Foundation

  5. Step 4 (Installments)

    • Supports installments: ON
    • Installment period: 12 months

  6. Step 5 (Images)

    Upload 2–3 placeholder images. Mark one as primary.

  7. Click Submit for Approval

Expected end of stage 1

Property exists with status pending_approval. It does NOT appear in the public investor listings yet. Owner sees the approval panel at the top of the property page showing "Awaiting admin review".

2.1 Things to verify at this stage

  • Confirmation message shown
  • Property visible in owner's My Properties as pending
  • Property NOT visible to investor accounts (open in another window to verify)
  • No on-chain activity yet (BSC Testnet shows nothing)

3. Stage 2: Admin approves

Browser session B — logged in as admin@capimax.com at /admin/.

  1. Django Admin → Properties → Property approvals

  2. Filter by status = submitted

  3. Open the Marina Tower 12 approval

  4. Review the headline fields against the documents

    For testing, everything is plausible; click Approve.

  5. Save

Expected end of stage 2

Property.status flips to approved. Owner receives a notification. Owner sees a "Tokenize this property" button on the property page.

3. Stage 3: Owner tokenizes (deploys contracts)

Back to owner session A.

  1. Refresh the property page — should now show "Approved" badge + Tokenize button

  2. Click Tokenize this property

  3. Review the deployment parameters dialog

    For an under-construction property, lockup is 730 days (2 years), category sent to factory as 0 (UNDER_CONSTRUCTION).

  4. Confirm. The progress indicator runs:

    • Calling PropertyContractFactory.deployProperty — tx 1
    • Factory calls clone.createProperty internally
    • Owner's UI calls factory.activateProperty — tx 2 (necessary because newly deployed properties start in DRAFT state)

  5. On success, copy the contract address from the page (e.g. 0xabc…)

Expected end of stage 3

Property.status = tokenized. Smart-contract address populated. Two BSCScan transactions visible (deployProperty + activateProperty). Property now appears in the public investor listings. No RentalIncomeDistributor was deployed (this is under-construction).

3.1 Verify on chain

Open the BSCScan link from the property page. You should see:

  • Contract creation tx (initiated by the factory)
  • PropertyDeployed event in the factory's logs
  • PropertyActivated event after the activate call

4. Stage 4: Investor A invests in installments

Browser session C — logged in as investor@test.com.

  1. Properties → find Marina Tower 12 (filter by Under Construction)

  2. Open the detail page. Confirm:

    • "Under Construction" badge visible
    • Expected completion date shown
    • Construction progress bar at 0%
    • "Installments available" indicator
    • NO monthly rental income card (correct — it's not built yet)

  3. Click Invest

  4. Step 1: Select Tokens. Enter 24 tokens ($2,880).

  5. Click Choose Schedule

    (The next-button label changes from "Choose Payment" to "Choose Schedule" because this property supports installments.)

  6. Step 2: Payment Schedule

    • "Pay upfront" card and "Pay in installments" card visible
    • Pick Pay in installments — expands the form
    • Select 4 installments. The card shows: "$720 per payment · $2,880 total"

  7. Click Choose Payment

  8. Step 3: Payment Method. Pick Credit Card.

    Tick the "I authorize recurring charges for installments" checkbox.

  9. Click Confirm & Pay

    Enter Stripe test card 4242 4242 4242 4242.

  10. Step 4: Processing. Watch the spinner.

    Behind the scenes:

    • Investment row created with status pending
    • First installment captured via Stripe
    • Backend mints 24 tokens with isInstallment=true → tokens go to ESCROW on the contract
    • Backend calls processInstallment for the first slice (6 tokens) → 6 tokens released to investor wallet

  11. Step 5: Confirmation. Shows "Installment 1 of 4 paid · 6 of 24 tokens released".

Expected end of stage 4

Investor A's wallet holds 6 tokens of property Marina Tower 12. Contract holds 18 tokens in escrow. Investment row exists in DB with linked ConstructionInstallment with payments_made = 1, total = 4. BSCScan shows: mintTokens (to escrow) + 1 processInstallment.

5. Stage 5: Investor B invests upfront

Browser session D — logged in as investor2@test.com.

  1. Open the same property page

  2. Click Invest

  3. Step 1: 50 tokens ($6,000)

  4. Click Choose Schedule

  5. Step 2: pick Pay upfront

    (Even though the property supports installments, this investor opts for upfront.)

  6. Click Choose Payment

  7. Step 3: pick Credit Card, enter test card

  8. Step 4: Processing

    Backend mints 50 tokens with isInstallment=false → tokens go directly to investor wallet.

  9. Step 5: Confirmation

Expected end of stage 5

Investor B's wallet holds 50 tokens. Property now has two investors. Contract balance: 18 in escrow (Investor A's unreleased) + 0 held by contract for Investor B = 18 total in escrow.

6. Stage 6: Owner adds milestones

Back to owner session A.

  1. Owner dashboard → My Properties → Marina Tower 12 → Construction tab

  2. Add milestone #1

    • Title: Foundation
    • Category: Foundation
    • Target date: 2 months from now
    • Progress: 100% (already complete)
    • Status: Completed

  3. Add milestone #2

    • Title: Framing & Structure
    • Category: Framing
    • Target date: 5 months from now
    • Progress: 30%
    • Status: In progress

  4. Add milestone #3

    • Title: Interior fit-out
    • Category: Interior
    • Target date: 10 months from now
    • Progress: 0%
    • Status: Not started

  5. Save

  6. Edit the overall property — bump construction_progress to 30% to reflect milestone 2's progress

Expected end of stage 6

Both investors see the construction-progress section populated with 3 milestones, current progress 30%, expected completion date visible. Investor A's holding detail also shows the milestones.

7. Stage 7: Subsequent installment payments

Switch to admin session B and trigger the remaining installments manually (this simulates the scheduled charge running on a monthly basis).

  1. Django Admin → Investments → Construction installment payments

  2. Filter by investor = Investor A & property = Marina Tower 12

  3. You see 4 rows: payments_made values 1, 2, 3, 4 in the schedule (only #1 marked paid so far)

  4. For the next unpaid installment, use the action menu → Process selected

    The platform charges the saved card, calls processInstallment on chain, releases another 6 tokens to Investor A.

  5. Verify Investor A's portfolio now shows 12 tokens (6 + 6)

  6. Repeat for installments 3 and 4

  7. After installment 4: Investor A has 24 tokens, escrow drops to 0 for this investor

Expected end of stage 7

Investor A has been issued 4 separate processInstallment transactions on chain, each releasing 6 tokens. ConstructionInstallment payments_made = 4, status = completed. Investor sees "Installments complete" badge in their portfolio.

8. Stage 8: Construction completes

Owner advances the property to completion over the course of testing.

  1. Owner marks milestone 2 (Framing) complete

  2. Owner marks milestone 3 (Interior) complete

  3. Owner edits property → sets construction_progress to 100%, construction_phase to "Complete"

  4. Optionally: owner adds a final "Construction complete" milestone and uploads photos

9. Stage 9: Property converts to income-producing

Once construction is complete, the owner converts the property to income-producing. This step is operationally significant.

  1. Owner edits property:

    • Set monthly_rental_income (e.g. 8000)
    • Set rental_distribution_frequency to Monthly
    • Set rental_income_active to ON
    • Optionally: change property_category to Ready Property (some workflows let you keep it as Under-construction-completed; check with your project lead)

  2. If the category change is supported, the platform now treats this property as a Ready property — rental distribution becomes available.

  3. Owner triggers their first rental distribution (see Stage 5 of Ready property workflow)

  4. Both investors A and B receive proportional dividends:

    • Investor A: 24/74 of net distribution (24 tokens of 74 total minted)
    • Investor B: 50/74 of net distribution

Expected end of stage 9

Property is now fully built, fully tokenized, generating income. Investors A and B both receive monthly dividends proportional to their token holdings. The full lifecycle is complete.

10. What can go wrong

Test each of the following failure modes:

StageFailureExpected platform behavior
3 (tokenize)Out of gasTokenization fails; property stays in approved; owner can retry
3 (tokenize)activateProperty call revertsProperty gets a contract address but mint won't work until manual recovery; admin sees this in monitoring
4 (installment invest)Stripe card declinedInvestment never created; no mint; investor sees clear error
4 (installment invest)Mint succeeds but processInstallment revertsTokens stuck in escrow; admin must investigate; refund pipeline can rescue
5 (upfront invest)Mint fails after 3 retriesInvestment status flips to mint_failed; auto-refund pipeline triggers; investor sees "refunded" status
7 (recurring installment)Saved card expiredInstallment fails; investor notified; given 7 days to update card before terminal failure
9 (rental distribution)Distribute called on a property still in under_construction category without conversionRefused on-chain with "Only for ready properties"

11.1 On-chain forensics

See On-chain verification for the full BSCScan walkthrough. Quick checks:

  • balanceOf(investorA_wallet, 0) on the property's contract should return 24
  • balanceOf(investorB_wallet, 0) should return 50
  • properties(0).status should be 1 (ACTIVE)
  • Event log should contain: 1 PropertyDeployed, 1 PropertyCreated, 1 PropertyActivated, 2 TokensMinted (one with isInstallment=true), 4 InstallmentPaid, 4 TokensGraduated, 1 RentalIncomeDistributed (after stage 9)
